My work spans browser-based environments, educational applications, interactive 3D tools, video-tooling experiments, and electronics projects. I enjoy exploring an idea, building a working version, and improving it through iteration.

I also contribute to projects under VRuntime Labs. RigCraft 3D, by contrast, is my personal project. I keep those project identities distinct.
